Mullion 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective: 2026-10-25

简体中文English

简体中文

Mullion（以下称“本应用”）是一款本地优先的收纳管理应用，由个人开发者 Ezlmt 开发。我们重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理数据。

1. 我们不收集你的个人数据

  • 本应用不需要注册或登录，没有账号系统。
  • 你录入的容器、物品、房间、备注、照片等全部内容只保存在你的设备上（应用私有存储），本应用不会把它们上传到任何服务器，开发者也无法访问。
  • 本应用没有广告，也没有接入任何第三方统计分析或广告 SDK。

2. 权限使用

  • 相机：用于拍摄物品和容器照片、连拍添加物品，以及 AR 测量容器尺寸。照片只保存在本机。
  • 照片选择：从相册选择照片时使用系统照片选择器；Android 12 及以下版本可能需要读取存储权限。所选照片会复制到应用私有目录，不会上传。
  • 网络：本应用自身不发送网络请求；网络仅用于下方第 3 条所述的 Google 组件（例如下载抠图模型）。

3. Google 提供的组件

  • Google ML Kit 主体分割（抠图）：通过 Google Play 服务在设备本地识别照片中的主体，图片不会上传。ML Kit 可能会向 Google 发送设备信息、应用信息、性能指标、错误码和安装标识符等诊断数据，用于诊断和改进服务；这些数据经 HTTPS 加密传输，详见 ML Kit 数据披露。
  • Google Play Services for AR（ARCore）：AR 测量功能基于 ARCore 运行。该服务由 Google 提供，并受 Google 隐私权政策约束。

4. 你主动分享的数据

当你使用“导出备份（JSON）”“导出清单（CSV）”“库存报告”或“分享标签”时，文件会通过系统分享面板发送到你选择的应用或位置。之后的处理由该应用或服务负责。

5. 系统备份

如果你在系统设置中开启了 Android 备份，系统可能把本应用的数据备份到你自己的 Google 账号。该功能由 Android/Google 提供，开发者无法访问这些备份。

6. 数据删除

你可以在应用内“设置”中重置或清空数据，或直接卸载本应用——卸载后本机上的所有应用数据会被删除。由于开发者不持有你的任何数据，无需另行申请删除。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用面向普通用户，不针对 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

8. 政策变更与联系方式

本政策如有更新，会发布在本页面并更新生效日期。如有疑问，请通过 GitHub @Ezlmt 或邮箱 ezlmttt@gmail.com 联系我们。

English

Mullion ("the app") is a local-first home storage organizer made by the independent developer Ezlmt. This policy explains how the app handles data.

1. We don't collect your personal data

  • The app needs no account and no sign-in.
  • Everything you enter (containers, items, rooms, notes, photos) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The app does not upload it anywhere, and the developer cannot access it.
  • The app has no ads and includes no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of items and containers, for Burst Add, and for AR measuring. Photos stay on your device.
  • Photo selection: the system photo picker is used; Android 12 and older may ask for storage access. Selected photos are copied into the app's private folder and are not uploaded.
  • Network: the app itself makes no network requests. Network access is only used by the Google components described in section 3 (for example to download the cut-out model).

3. Components provided by Google

  • Google ML Kit subject segmentation (cut-out) runs on your device through Google Play services; your photos are not uploaded. ML Kit may send diagnostic data to Google, such as device information, app information, performance metrics, error codes and per-installation identifiers, to diagnose and improve the service. This data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). See the ML Kit data disclosure.
  • Google Play Services for AR (ARCore): AR measuring runs on ARCore, which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data you choose to share

When you export a backup (JSON), an inventory list (CSV), an inventory report, or share labels, the file is sent through the system share sheet to the app or place you choose. That destination's own policy applies from then on.

5. System backup

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, the system may back up the app's data to your own Google account. This is an Android/Google feature; the developer has no access to these backups.

6. Deleting your data

You can reset or clear your data in the app's Settings, or uninstall the app, which deletes all of its data from your device. The developer holds none of your data, so there is nothing else to request.

7. Children

The app is meant for a general audience, is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: contact us via GitHub @Ezlmt or email ezlmttt@gmail.com.
